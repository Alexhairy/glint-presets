Optiq privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Optiq is a Windows app made by Alexhairy, an individual developer in Perth, Australia.

What Optiq collects

Nothing. Optiq has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and it doesn't send your settings, files or any personal information anywhere.

What stays on your PC

Your profiles, keybinds and settings are saved on your own computer. If you connect Optiq to OBS, the OBS password you paste in is saved encrypted for your Windows account and is only ever sent to OBS on your own PC.

When Optiq uses the internet

  • The Library page downloads the public list of presets from GitHub (github.com). Like any website visit, GitHub sees the request (your IP address and that a download happened). Optiq sends nothing else. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • The Microsoft Store handles buying Optiq, free trials, passes and updates. Microsoft's privacy policy applies to those.

Diagnostics report

The "Copy report" button copies a summary of your display setup to your clipboard so you can choose to send it for help. It lists things like your graphics card, driver version, Windows version and monitors, and the results of Optiq's checks. Nothing is sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Children

Optiq doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
